# Karmi tester guide (first tester release)

Karmi is a test build of an AI study helper for students: chat in English, Hindi or a mix. Replies
come from free AI models and can be wrong. Check anything important.

1. Install the APK (Android only), open it, read and accept the tester notice.
2. Sign in with your email: Clerk sends a code to your email; enter it.
3. Ask things you would ask a tutor: explain a topic, solve a maths problem, debug a short
   program, summarise a passage, or write in Hindi / Hinglish.
4. Under each reply use thumbs up or thumbs down (pick a reason on thumbs down) and Regenerate if
   the answer is poor. Regenerate tries again and may use a stronger model.
5. Do not type passwords, bank/ID numbers, or other people's private information.

Limits: each plan has a daily request and token allowance (Ananta 20 requests/day; usage screen
shows what is left). Free models are rate-limited by their providers, so you may see "I couldn't
reach the AI service" at busy times: try again later. That does not use up your allowance.

Report problems with a screenshot, the time, and what you typed (without personal data).
Known limitations are in `known-issues.md`.
