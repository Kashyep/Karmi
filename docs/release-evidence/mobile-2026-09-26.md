## Device failures found and fixed (each has a test that failed before the fix)

| # | Failure on device | Test that reproduces it | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Enabled send icon invisible in glass shell | `device_regressions_test.dart` "enabled send icon contrasts with its fill (glass shell)" | Material `IconTheme` restored under `GlassScaffold` |
| 2 | Glass app bar had no platter; title illegible over content | "glass app bar has a tint platter at the legibility floor" (light, dark) | `GlassAppBar.backgroundColor = KarmiGlass.tint(...)` |
| 3 | Toggling reduce transparency / contrast erased the chat transcript | "toggling reduce transparency keeps the transcript and tab" | `GlobalKey` on the tab `IndexedStack` |
| 4 | Licences node swallowed its card (wrong TalkBack order) | "settings card exposes each control as its own node in order" | `KarmiCard(semanticContainer: false)` in Settings |
| 5 | Glass overflow menu items missing from the accessibility tree | "overflow menu items are reachable by screen readers" (checks the real semantics tree) | Material `PopupMenuButton` in both shells |
| 6 | No visible keyboard focus: switch, tiles, glass tabs, theme segments, filled button; touch highlight mode during key traversal | `keyboard_focus_test.dart` (8 tests: ring pixels per control, per-segment ring, filled-button change, touch-mode ring, no ring after a tap) | `KarmiFocusRing`; theme choice as three buttons; filled-button inset ring |
| 6b | Theme choice lost "selected" after fix 6 | "theme choices expose selected state to screen readers" | `Semantics(selected:)` inside each button |
